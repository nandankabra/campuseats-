# CampusEats Brief

## What the System Does

CampusEats is an online food-ordering platform designed for a college or university campus. It allows students to browse menus from campus cafeterias and nearby restaurants, place food orders, make payments, and track the status of their orders. The system helps students get food conveniently while helping food vendors manage orders efficiently.

## Who Uses the System

The primary users of CampusEats are:

1. **Students** – Browse menus, place orders, make payments, and track deliveries.
2. **Restaurant/Cafeteria Staff** – Manage menus, receive orders, prepare food, and update order status.
3. **Delivery Personnel** – Pick up orders and deliver them to students.
4. **Administrators** – Manage users, restaurants, menus, and monitor system activity.

CampusEats is a campus-focused food ordering and delivery system that connects students with food providers. It simplifies food ordering, payment processing, order tracking, and delivery management while providing an efficient platform for restaurants and administrators to manage their operations.
